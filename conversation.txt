● I found the actual source logic for $stock and the tier column — and it
  changes the picture significantly. Let me walk through it before touching any
  code.

  The tier column isn't a wealth/quality tier at all. It's called era in the
  original schema, and it's a ruleset/tech-level gate, not a wealth gate:

  - For D&D (game='DD') — the game your generator actually uses — era is never 
  even checked. The query is just WHERE game='DD' AND store='Tavern'
  (city_fantasy.php:678 (wizardawn-source/functions/city_fantasy.php#L678), same
  in city_gn.php:678 (wizardawn-source/functions/city_gn.php#L678)).
  - For Tunnels & Trolls, era = Simple/Complex/Deluxe/Deluxes selects which T&T 
  rulebook edition is active (city_tt.php:765-771
  (wizardawn-source/functions/city_tt.php#L765-L771)) — "Deluxe" vs "Deluxes" is
  two intentionally distinct values (old vs new Deluxe-edition rules), not a
  typo. (Dweluxe, the single stray occurrence, does look like a genuine
  data-entry typo of "Deluxe".)
  - For post-apocalyptic, era = low/hi/both gates by settlement tech level —
  clubs vs laser guns (city_post_apocalyptic.php:429-430
  (wizardawn-source/functions/city_post_apocalyptic.php#L429-L430)), driven by a
  $tech flag the DM picks, matching the "high tech settlement or low tech one"
  UI text I found in tool_mtown.php.
  - Blank era = universal, works under any gate.

  $stock has no wealth logic either. It's a flat DM-picked dropdown (5%–100% in
  5% steps) on the town-generation form, and the only thing it does is: for each
  catalog item, mt_rand(1,100) <= $stock decides in/out (city_fantasy.php:684
  (wizardawn-source/functions/city_fantasy.php#L684)). No connection to
  city_size or economy anywhere in source.

  What does scale with settlement size/wealth: $city_size → $economy (1/2/3 for
  ≤400/≤800/>800) → how many rounds of buildings get generated — one pass of 14
  shop types per economy point, plus separate rounds for guild halls, banks,
  guards, and church (fantasy_stores_osric.php:5-9
  (wizardawn-source/functions/fantasy_stores_osric.php#L5-L9)). That's the real
  "settlement wealth" mechanic in the source — it controls how many buildings a
  town gets, not how full each shop's shelves are.

  So: our earlier agreement ("inventories scale with settlement wealth") isn't
  actually how the original worked — it'd be new mechanics we invent, same
  category as the already-flagged MAGIC_SELL_TABLE placeholder. Meanwhile I
  stumbled onto the actual, real, previously-undiscovered settlement-scaling
  formula, which is exactly the missing piece for "building-by-building"
  generation and directly answers the harder question from our last
  conversation.
